# 30 Ideas to Enhance Your Psychedelic Shooter

Here's a checklist of diverse ideas to potentially add more layers and fun to your game:

Griffin Ideas:

- Real enemies with AI
- Cooler teleport animation
- More music
- Add a confirm selection button for evolution
- Settings page

## Gameplay Mechanics & Systems
- [x] **Weapon Evolution**: Have the weapon system dynamically morph or evolve (triggered by combos, energy fragments, or score thresholds).
- [ ] **Reality Glitch Power-ups**: Collectible items that trigger temporary shifts in game reality (Color Inversion, Time Dilation, Phasing, Entity Swap).
- [ ] **Rhythm-Based Bonuses**: Integrate gameplay elements with the audio track, rewarding actions in time with musical cues.
- [ ] **Shield/Absorption Mechanic**: Defensive energy shield that absorbs enemy fire and releases powerful blasts.
- [ ] **Dimension Shift**: Allow player to shift into alternate perceptual dimensions with different gameplay implications.
- [ ] **Symbiotic Enemies**: Enemy types that are explicitly linked, where destroying one affects others.
- [ ] **Environmental Hazards**: Dynamic threats beyond enemies (Shifting Geometric Structures, Energy Fields, Reality Tears).
- [ ] **Color-Coded Enemies/Damage**: System where enemies have color affiliations that affect vulnerability to different weapon types.
- [ ] **Gravity Wells/Distortions**: Localized zones with warped physics affecting movement and projectiles.
- [ ] **Mind-Bending Boss Fights**: Encounters against abstract entities with phases that warp the visual environment.

## Visuals & Audio
- [ ] **Audio-Reactive Environment**: Make background patterns and visual elements react to sound and music.
- [ ] **Shader Effects**: Implement post-processing shaders for visual feedback (Chromatic Aberration, Pixelation, Bloom).
- [ ] **Trail Customization**: Allow players to personalize the visual trail left by their plane.
- [ ] **Dynamic Music System**: Adaptive music that shifts based on gameplay intensity.
- [ ] **Expressive Enemy Designs**: Enhanced visual communication through color changes, charging animations, and death effects.
- [ ] **Warp Speed/Tunnel Effect**: Visual effect for high-speed moments or transitions between zones.
- [ ] **Surreal Soundscape**: Abstract and unconventional sounds for weapons, impacts, and ambient atmosphere.

## Content & World
- [ ] **Procedurally Generated Levels**: Dynamic level structures combining pre-designed segments.
- [ ] **Themed Zones**: Distinct regions with unique visual identity and gameplay elements.
- [ ] **Narrative Fragments**: Subtle storytelling elements scattered throughout the game.
- [ ] **Hidden Collectibles/Secrets**: Rewards for exploration and completing specific triggers.
- [ ] **Enemy Variety & Behavior**: Wider range of adversaries with unique movement and attack patterns.
- [ ] **Friendly Entities**: Non-hostile entities that provide benefits or assistance.

## Controls & UI
- [ ] **Enhanced HUD**: UI elements integrated into the psychedelic aesthetic.
- [ ] **Haptic Feedback**: Tactile feedback for game events through controller vibration.
- [ ] **"Focus" Mode**: Limited-use ability to temporarily slow down perceived time.
- [ ] **After-Image Aiming**: Visual echo of crosshair position to help with aiming.

## Meta & Progression
- [ ] **Unlockable Plane Skins/Trails**: Cosmetic modifications unlocked through gameplay achievements.
- [ ] **Difficulty Modes**: Different difficulty settings adjusting game variables.
- [ ] **Persistent Upgrades**: Light progression system between game runs for permanent improvements.
